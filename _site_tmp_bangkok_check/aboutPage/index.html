I enjoy building digital experiences that combine structure with creativity.

Over the past few years, I have worked across web development, backend logic, testing, APIs, databases, and quality-focused processes, which gave me a broad technical foundation and a good understanding of how different parts of a product connect. What motivates me most is creating things that are not only technically solid, but also thoughtful, useful, and well designed for the people who use them.

At the same time, I prefer to be honest about where I stand: I have explored and used many technologies, but I would not describe myself as someone who already knows one stack so deeply that I can move through everything with complete confidence. I am curious, committed, and ready to learn, and I would love to grow further in an environment where mentorship, collaboration, and clear guidance are part of the culture. I feel most at home in international, open-minded teams where questions are welcome, ideas can be shared freely, and there is space to keep developing both technically and personally.
